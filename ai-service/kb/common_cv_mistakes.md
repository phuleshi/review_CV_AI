# Common CV Mistakes — V1

Catalogue of frequent CV problems the AI should detect, with the fix to recommend.
Each item maps to a scoring category so feedback ties back to the rubric.

## Content mistakes

| # | Mistake | Why it hurts | Fix to suggest | Category |
|---|---------|--------------|----------------|----------|
| 1 | Duty-listing ("Responsible for…") | Shows tasks, not impact | Rewrite as action + result + metric | experience |
| 2 | No quantified achievements | Recruiters can't gauge scale | Add numbers: %, $, users, time saved | experience |
| 3 | Generic objective ("seeking a challenging role") | Wastes prime space | Replace with a targeted 2–3 line summary | structure |
| 4 | Skills never backed up | Reads as a wish-list | Demonstrate each key skill in experience/projects | skills |
| 5 | Soft-skill padding ("hardworking, team player") | Unverifiable filler | Cut or show via evidence | skills |
| 6 | Tutorial-clone projects only | No signal of real ability | Add original/complex projects with your role | projects |
| 7 | Projects with no outcome or link | Can't be evaluated | Add repo/demo link + result | projects |
| 8 | Irrelevant content for the target role | Dilutes focus | Tailor to the role/JD | skills/ats |

## Writing mistakes

| # | Mistake | Why it hurts | Fix to suggest | Category |
|---|---------|--------------|----------------|----------|
| 9  | Passive voice | Weak, vague | Lead bullets with action verbs | experience |
| 10 | First person / pronouns ("I led…") | Non-standard for CVs | Drop pronouns: "Led…" | structure |
| 11 | Inconsistent tense | Looks careless | Past for old roles, present for current | structure |
| 12 | Typos / grammar errors | Signals low attention | Proofread; run a checker | structure |
| 13 | Walls of text | Not scannable | Convert to concise bullets | structure |

## Formatting / ATS mistakes

| # | Mistake | Why it hurts | Fix to suggest | Category |
|---|---------|--------------|----------------|----------|
| 14 | Multi-column layout | Breaks ATS parsing order | Switch to single column | ats |
| 15 | Contact info only in header/footer | Often dropped by ATS | Put it in the body | ats |
| 16 | Photo / graphics / icons | Parsing noise; bias risk | Remove unless required by region | ats |
| 17 | CV exported as image/scan | Unreadable by ATS | Export as text-based PDF | ats |
| 18 | Creative section names | ATS can't categorize | Use standard headings | ats |
| 19 | > 2 pages with little experience | Recruiters skim | Trim to 1–2 pages | structure |
| 20 | Inconsistent date formats | Looks sloppy, parses poorly | Standardize `MMM YYYY` | structure/ats |

## Honesty / red flags

| # | Mistake | Why it hurts | Fix to suggest | Category |
|---|---------|--------------|----------------|----------|
| 21 | Self-rated skill bars ("Python 95%") | Meaningless, unverifiable | Replace with evidence | skills |
| 22 | Unexplained employment gaps | Raises questions | Briefly note (study, freelance, caregiving) | experience |
| 23 | Buzzword stuffing | Reads as keyword spam | Keep keywords contextual | skills/ats |
| 24 | Outdated tech front-and-center | Signals stale skillset | Lead with current, relevant tech | skills |

## How the AI should use this file

- When a mistake is detected, phrase it in `weaknesses` and put the concrete fix in `suggestions`.
- Prefer the highest-impact 3–6 issues over an exhaustive list.
- Never fabricate a mistake that isn't supported by the CV text.
