# Feedback Templates — V1

Reusable phrasing so AI feedback is consistent, specific, and actionable. These are
**patterns to adapt**, not canned strings to paste verbatim. Always ground feedback
in the actual CV.

## Tone

- Constructive and direct. Professional, encouraging, never harsh.
- Specific over generic: name the section/line and the fix.
- Action-oriented: every weakness should have a matching suggestion.
- Second person ("your experience section…") in `summary`; concise fragments in lists.

## Strengths (patterns)

- "Strong quantified impact — e.g. {{example metric}} clearly shows scale."
- "Skills section is well-organized and relevant to {{target role}}."
- "Projects include {{repo/demo}}, making your work verifiable."
- "Clear, single-column layout that is easy to scan and ATS-friendly."
- "Consistent, action-led bullets across the experience section."

## Weaknesses (patterns)

- "Experience bullets describe duties rather than outcomes (e.g. '{{quote}}')."
- "Several listed skills ({{skills}}) aren't backed up in experience or projects."
- "No measurable results — recruiters can't gauge the impact of your work."
- "{{Multi-column / table / image}} layout risks breaking ATS parsing."
- "Missing a professional summary, so the target role isn't immediately clear."

## Suggestions (patterns)

- "Rewrite '{{weak bullet}}' as 'action + result + metric', e.g. '{{example}}'."
- "Quantify achievements: add %, $, user counts, or time saved."
- "Add a 2–3 line summary at the top tailored to {{role}}."
- "Switch to a single-column layout with standard headings for ATS."
- "Add a repo or live-demo link to your {{project}} project."
- "Group skills into categories (languages / frameworks / tools)."

## Summary (template)

> "A {{seniority}} CV scoring {{overall}}/100. Strongest in {{top category}}; the
> biggest wins are {{1–2 highest-impact fixes}}. Address these to move toward
> {{next band}}."

Example:
> "A solid mid-level CV scoring 74/100. Strongest in skills; the biggest wins are
> quantifying your experience bullets and adding project links. Address these to push
> into the strong (85+) band."

## Suggestion quality bar

- Each suggestion is **independently actionable** — the candidate knows exactly what to change.
- Prefer 3–6 high-impact suggestions over a long shallow list.
- Reference the CV's own content; never invent examples that contradict it.
- Order by impact (highest-scoring opportunity first).
